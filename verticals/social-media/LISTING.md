## Description (2,000 characters at most)

Windsor.ai for Social Media is a workspace for social media managers and the agencies that run social for their clients. It connects your own social accounts, Instagram, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn company pages, Threads, TikTok, YouTube and Google Business Profile, and nothing else, so Claude stays on social media work.

Publish after you approve: Reels, image, carousel and story posts on Instagram; text, link and photo posts on Facebook Pages and LinkedIn Pages; text and image posts on Threads; local posts with a call-to-action button on Google Business Profile. One message can be adapted and cross-posted to several accounts.

Handle the community: find Instagram comments and Threads replies the account has not answered and reply to them, hide or delete spam on Instagram, and answer Google reviews that have no owner reply yet.

Keep the Google Business Profile listing current: opening hours, holiday hours, temporary closure, description, website, phone numbers, categories, attributes, services, service area and photos.

Report on what works: reach, views, engagement, follower growth and top posts per platform; Reels watch time and skip rate on Instagram, Reels plays on Facebook, Shorts on YouTube; Google Business Profile views, calls and direction requests.

Claude shows every post, reply and listing change and waits for your yes before it runs. TikTok and YouTube are read-only, and Facebook and TikTok comments can be read but not answered.

Pair it with the Windsor.ai for Social Media plugin for Claude Code and Cowork, which adds slash commands such as /publish-post, /comment-inbox and /review-replies and a read-only Social Media Analyst agent.

Windsor.ai is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the platforms it connects to.
